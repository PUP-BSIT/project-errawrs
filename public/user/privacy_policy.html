Privacy Policy

Maintaining your privacy is an important part of all the products and services that StackOvercash provides. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, protect, use, and share information when you access any of our digital platforms.

What We May Collect From You

  • Your name and personal particulars such as contact details, birthdate, email
  • Your account information and details, such as account number, balances, or transaction history
  • Your government-issued IDs and digital photos
  • Your username, password, PIN, biometrics, or other authentication credentials
  • Device identifier for the device running our app
  • Personal and non-personal information combined

How We Use Your Information

  • To identify and authorize your log-in and authenticate transactions
  • To recognize your device for a more personalized experience
  • To process your transactions and provide services
  • To improve our products, services, and customer support
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • To communicate important updates, offers, and service information (with your consent)

How We Protect Your Information

  • We use encryption, secure servers, and regular security audits
  • Access to your information is strictly limited to authorized personnel
  • We require our service providers to protect your data aligned with our standards

How Long Do We Keep Your Information

  • Your personal information is retained as long as necessary for the purposes it was collected
  • We securely dispose of your data when it is no longer required for legal, regulatory, or business purposes

Your Rights

  • Request information about what we do with your personal data
  • Access, update, or request deletion of your personal information
  • Withdraw consent for data processing (where applicable)
  • Request to not receive marketing materials
  • Lodge a complaint regarding our use of your data

How to Contact Us

For any queries, clarifications, comments, or requests regarding this Policy or your personal data, please contact our Data Protection Officer at stackovercash.site@gmail.com.
